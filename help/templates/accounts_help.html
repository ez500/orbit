Accounts

To access most of the tools on this platform you will need an account.

Making an Account

Here is how to make an account.

Creation

  • Click the Login button on the sidebar.
  • Click the Register button on Login Page.
  • Fill in the required information and then click Signup.

Personalizing Account

You can change details about your accoutn and customize preferences to your liking.

Change Settings

  • Click the Profile button on the sidebar.
  • Click the Settings button on Profile Page.

Change Profile

  • Click the Profile button on the sidebar.
  • Click the Edit Profile button on Profile Page.
